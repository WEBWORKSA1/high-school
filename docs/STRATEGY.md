# High.School — Idea, Positioning & Revenue Model

## The verdict
**Build High.School as "the free command center for high school": tools + study hubs + college/diploma pathways, with a lead-gen funnel aimed at parents and adult learners.**

Not a school directory (Niche/GreatSchools own that, and it needs licensed data). Not a homework-answers site (Chegg/Brainly are bleeding from AI). The domain is a category-defining exact-match generic, so it should own the *category hub* — the page a student bookmarks and a parent trusts.

## Why this idea wins (numbers)
| Lever | Data point | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Audience size | US public school enrollment, grades 9–12: **~15.6M** (NCES, fall 2023) — plus parents, private-school, homeschool and international students | Massive, evergreen, refreshes every year (a new freshman class every September) |
| AdSense value | Education RPM commonly cited at **~$8–25** for US traffic; online-education CPCs **$3–16+** | Mid-to-high value niche; tools create repeat pageviews |
| Lead value | Education CPL typically **$25–70**; higher-ed inquiries average **~$128** | Lead gen out-earns AdSense 5–10x per engaged user |
| Seasonality | FAFSA opens Oct 1 · Early apps Nov 1 · Regular apps Jan 1 · AP exams May · SAT/ACT dates monthly | Built-in content calendar and traffic spikes |

## The flaw you must design around
**Teens are a weak AdSense audience.** Google does not serve personalized ads to users it knows are under 18, which drags RPM down. Leads from minors are also legally risky (COPPA under 13; TCPA/state privacy for under-18 contact data).

**Fix:** build the money pages for **parents** and **adult learners (18+)**:
- Parent Hub, Financial Aid / FAFSA, College Cost calculator → high-CPC parent traffic
- Online High School & Diploma Completion guide → the single most valuable lead page (adults finishing a diploma + families switching to online/credit recovery)
- Every lead form asks "I am a: student 13–17 / parent-guardian / adult learner 18+ / educator". Under-13 is blocked. 13–17 needs parent/guardian confirmation.

## Revenue stack (in order of $ per visitor)
1. **Lead gen** — "Get Matched": tutoring · test prep · college counseling · online diploma/credit recovery · scholarship alerts. Sell/refer leads to vetted partners (CPL or rev-share).
2. **AdSense** — Auto ads on long-form guides, tools and video pages. No ads on forms/legal pages.
3. **Sponsorships** — contests, scholarships and tool pages "presented by" a brand ($500–5,000 per placement).
4. **Affiliate** (phase 2) — test-prep courses, calculators, dorm/college gear.
5. **Donations** — credibility + operating budget; transparent allocation (ops, marketing, hiring, contest prizes).

## Illustrative model (assumptions, not promises)
| Monthly | Conservative | Bold |
|---|---|---|
| Pageviews | 100,000 | 1,000,000 |
| AdSense @ $10 RPM / $15 RPM | $1,000 | $15,000 |
| Users → lead rate 1% / 2% | 400 leads | 8,000 leads |
| Lead value $15 / $30 | $6,000 | $240,000 |
| Sponsorships | $0 | $10,000 |

**Takeaway:** AdSense pays the hosting; the lead funnel is the business. Treat the GPA calculator as top-of-funnel and Get Matched as the product.

## Traffic engines (build first, they rank)
1. GPA calculator (weighted/unweighted)
2. Final grade calculator ("what do I need on my final")
3. SAT ↔ ACT score converter
4. Grade/category calculator
5. College cost & savings calculator
6. Credit/graduation tracker
7. Study timer (Pomodoro) + flashcards (repeat-visit loops)

## Bold path vs. safe path
- **Safe:** AdSense + email list + slow content growth. ~12–18 months to meaningful revenue.
- **Bold:** Sign 2–3 online high school / tutoring partners on CPL in month 1, push "Get Matched" on every page, run a sponsored monthly contest to build the email list and backlinks. Revenue in 60–90 days if traffic lands.
