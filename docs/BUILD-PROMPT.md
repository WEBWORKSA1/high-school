## PHASE 1 — Foundation & architecture
> Create the repo skeleton: `build.py`, `src/` (content modules), `assets/css/site.css`, `assets/js/site.js`, `assets/img/`, `docs/`. `build.py` must expose `page(path, title, description, body, crumbs, schema, ads=True)` that writes `path/index.html` with: meta title/description, canonical (`BASE_URL` constant = `https://high.school`), Open Graph/Twitter tags, JSON-LD (WebSite + Organization on home; BreadcrumbList on every page; FAQPage/HowTo/Article where relevant), favicon SVG, web manifest, AdSense script, Google Fonts, the top partnership bar, header, footer, cookie banner, lead slide-in. It must also emit `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `ads.txt`, `search-index.json`, `404.html`, `.nojekyll`.
> **Accept when:** `python3 build.py` produces every page, and a link-checker finds zero broken relative links.

## PHASE 2 — Design system
> Build a distinctive "study notebook" identity: paper background, ink-navy text, highlighter-yellow accent, coral for CTAs, teal for success. Display serif (Fraunces) + clean sans (Inter). Components: buttons, cards, chips, tabs, accordion (FAQ), progress bar, stepper form, stat tiles, tables, video "lite" embed (thumbnail → iframe on click, youtube-nocookie), toast, modal, breadcrumb, mega-menu, mobile drawer, dark mode toggle (saved in localStorage, wrapped in try/catch).
> **Accept when:** a `/style-check` render at 375px and 1280px shows no overflow and every component meets AA contrast in light and dark.

## PHASE 3 — Core pages
> Home (hero with search + "What do you need today?" intent chips; tools strip; subject grid; roadmap teaser by grade 9–12; video row; Get Matched CTA band; contests/support teaser; newsletter), About, Contact (form), Search (client-side over `search-index.json`), Thank-you, 404.

## PHASE 5 — Interactive tools (traffic engines)

## PHASE 6 — Lead generation (the business)
> `/get-matched/`: 4-step form with progress bar — (1) I am a: student 13–17 / parent-guardian / adult learner 18+ / educator; (2) I need: tutoring · test prep · college counseling · online diploma/credit recovery · scholarship alerts · summer programs; (3) details: grade, subjects, timeline, budget range, country, ZIP/postal; (4) contact: name, email, phone (optional), preferred contact time, consent checkbox (+ parent/guardian checkbox for 13–17). Block under-13. Trust strip (free, no spam, unsubscribe anytime), FAQ, "what happens next" timeline. Inline mini-forms on every content page, a dismissible slide-in after engagement (once per session), and context CTAs on tools ("Your GPA is 3.2 — get a free tutoring match").
> Submission: AJAX POST to FormSubmit using the runtime-assembled email; honeypot; `_subject` tagged by lead type; redirect to thank-you.
> **Accept when:** a test submission arrives in the inbox with all fields and a lead-type subject line, and the email address does not appear in page source.
