# Competitive teardown — 47 sites fetched (October 2026)

How the build used this research: the GPA tool got weighting, cumulative, target planner and autosave. The lead funnel became a role-first multi-step match form with progress bar, "no obligation" trust strip and an under-18 guardian path. Scholarship alerts state their email volume up front. Contests follow the sweepstakes model with official rules and a free entry route. The online-diploma guide got its own advisor flow. Every test-prep page carries the trademark disclaimer. The home page has an audience chooser (Students / Parents / Educators / Adult learners). Tool pages show an updated date and say results are estimates.

## Must-have features (recurring across the best sites)
- **Content:** subject hubs, topic guides, practice questions with explanations, score explainers, test-date cards, common-mistake callouts, scholarship lists by class year, career profiles, online-school explainers, video course grids, downloadable lead magnets.
- **Tools:** GPA (weighted, cumulative, target), grade-calculator family, AP score calculators, SAT-vs-ACT quiz, career and college quizzes, cost/net-price tools, school finder, flashcards, scholarship tracker.
- **Lead gen:** one-tap qualifier first, role-first branching (student / parent / educator), progress bar, "No obligation · ~1 minute", consent language, a parent path for minors, stated email volume.
- **Community:** contests and sweepstakes with official rules, winners pages, saved items, donations.
- **Trust:** trademark disclaimers, age gates, "updated" dates, estimate disclaimers, disclosure of paid placement, privacy controls.
- **Design:** search-first or audience-chooser hero, numbered how-it-works steps, subject tile grids, outcome testimonials, big footer directories.

## Avoid
- Guilt-trip "no thanks" popups
- Pre-checked consent to sell personal data
- Collecting minors' phone numbers without a guardian path
