Accessibility Statement

Effective October 2026.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

We want High.School to be usable by everyone. Our goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic HTML with headings, landmarks and a “skip to content” link.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators.
  • Labels for every form field and live regions for results and form status.
  • Color contrast checked in light and dark modes; a dark mode toggle in the header.
  • Respect for “reduced motion” system settings.
  • Videos load only when you choose to play them.

Known limitations

Embedded third-party videos and ads may not fully meet our standards. Video captions are provided by their creators on YouTube.

Feedback

If you find a barrier, please tell us through our contact form. We aim to respond within five business days.
