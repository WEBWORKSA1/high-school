Cookie Policy

Effective October 2026.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. We also use local storage, a similar browser feature, to save your tool data and preferences on your device.

What we use

TypePurposeSet by
Essential local storageRemembers your theme, cookie choice, and tool data (GPA courses, flashcards, checklists)High.School (stays on your device)
Advertising cookiesServe and measure ads, limit repetition, and (if allowed) personalize adsGoogle and its partners via AdSense
Video cookiesOnly set when you click to play a YouTube videoYouTube (privacy-enhanced mode)
FontsDeliver web fontsGoogle Fonts

Your choices

  • Use our cookie notice to choose Accept all or Non-personalized only. To reset your choice, clear this site’s data in your browser.
  • Manage personalized Google ads at Google Ads Settings and other vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings (some features may stop working).
