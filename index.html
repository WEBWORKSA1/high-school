Frequently asked questions

Is High.School free?

Yes. Every guide, tool, quiz and video page is free. We’re supported by advertising, optional partner referrals and reader donations.

Who is High.School for?

Students aged 13+, parents and guardians, adult learners finishing a diploma, and teachers and counselors.

Is High.School a school?

No. High.School is an independent educational resource. We don’t grant diplomas or credits; our guides help you choose accredited programs and succeed in your own school.

How does “Get Matched” work?

Tell us your goal in four quick steps. We reply with free resources and, if you want, introductions to vetted tutoring, test-prep, counseling or online-school options. No obligation.
