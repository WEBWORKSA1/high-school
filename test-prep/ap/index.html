🎓 AP Courses & Exams Guide

How AP courses and exams work — scoring 1–5, college credit, how many APs to take, exam timing in May and proven study strategies.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

Advanced Placement (AP) courses are college-level classes taught in high school. Each ends with an exam in May, scored 1 to 5. Many colleges grant credit or placement for scores of 3, 4 or 5 — which can save tuition and let you start in higher-level courses.

How AP scoring works

ScoreMeaning
5Extremely well qualified
4Very well qualified
3Qualified
2Possibly qualified
1No recommendation

Most exams combine a multiple-choice section and a free-response section. Most AP exams are now taken digitally in the Bluebook app, while some are hybrid (answers to free-response questions handwritten). Scores are typically released in early July.

Popular AP courses by interest

  • STEM: Calculus AB/BC, Statistics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics 1/2/C, Computer Science A & Principles, Environmental Science
  • Humanities: English Language, English Literature, US History, World History, European History, Art History
  • Social sciences: Psychology, US Government, Comparative Government, Macroeconomics, Microeconomics, Human Geography
  • Languages & arts: Spanish, French, Chinese, Latin, Music Theory, Studio Art, AP Seminar & Research (Capstone)

How many AP classes should I take?

Quality beats quantity. Colleges evaluate rigor relative to what your school offers. A common pattern is 0–1 AP in 10th grade, 2–3 in 11th and 2–4 in 12th — chosen around your strengths and intended major. Overloading to the point that grades drop or sleep disappears is a net negative. Use the GPA calculator to see how weighted AP grades affect your GPA.

AP study strategy

  1. Download the course and exam description (CED) for each course — it lists every unit and skill.
  2. Practice past free-response questions; College Board publishes many with scoring guidelines.
  3. Score your own responses using the rubric — it teaches you what readers reward.
  4. From March, do one timed mixed MCQ set per week per course.
  5. Use AP Classroom progress checks your teacher assigns — they mirror exam style.

Frequently asked questions

Do AP scores go on my college application?

You may self-report AP scores on most applications. Policies vary; some students choose to report only strong scores. Colleges see your AP course grades on your transcript regardless.

Can I take an AP exam without taking the class?

Yes, you can self-study and register through a school that offers the exam. Contact the school’s AP coordinator early (usually by fall).

Do all colleges give credit for a 3?

No. Credit policies vary by college and even by department. Search “[college name] AP credit policy” for the details.
