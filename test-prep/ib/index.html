🌐 IB Diploma Programme Guide

How the International Baccalaureate Diploma works — six subject groups, HL vs SL, TOK, the Extended Essay, CAS and the 45-point scoring scale.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

The International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma Programme is a rigorous two-year curriculum for students aged 16–19, offered at thousands of schools worldwide. Unlike AP (individual courses), the IB Diploma is a complete program with a shared core.

Structure

  • Six subjects, one from each group: Language & Literature, Language Acquisition, Individuals & Societies, Sciences, Mathematics, and the Arts (or a second subject from another group).
  • Usually three at Higher Level (HL) and three at Standard Level (SL).
  • The core: Theory of Knowledge (TOK), the Extended Essay (an independent research paper of up to 4,000 words) and Creativity, Activity, Service (CAS).

Scoring

ComponentPoints
6 subjects × 7 points42
TOK + Extended Essay (combined)up to 3
Maximum45

A minimum of 24 points (plus other conditions, such as completing CAS) is required to earn the diploma. Results are released in early July for May exams.

Is IB right for you?

IB suits students who like interdisciplinary thinking, writing and long-term projects. The workload is sustained over two years rather than concentrated in exam season. Universities worldwide recognize the diploma, and many grant credit for strong HL scores.

Survival tips

  1. Start the Extended Essay topic search early in year one — pick a question you genuinely care about.
  2. Log CAS experiences weekly; don’t reconstruct them at the end.
  3. Use subject guides and past papers with mark schemes.
  4. Protect sleep — IB is a marathon.

Frequently asked questions

IB vs AP — which is better?

Neither is universally better. IB is an integrated program with a research and service core; AP lets you pick individual courses. Colleges value both when taken at a high level.

Can I take IB courses without the full diploma?

Yes, many schools let students take individual IB courses and earn course certificates.
