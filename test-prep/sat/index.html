📝 The Digital SAT: Complete Guide

Everything about the digital SAT — sections, timing, adaptive modules, scoring, what’s a good score, and an 8-week study plan.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

The SAT is a college admission test used by most US colleges and many international universities. Since 2024 it has been fully digital and adaptive: you take it on a laptop or tablet in the official Bluebook app at a test center, and the second module of each section adjusts to how you did on the first.

What’s on each section

Reading & Writing

Short passages — usually a single paragraph — each with one question. Skills tested: information and ideas (main idea, evidence, inferences), craft and structure (vocabulary in context, text structure), expression of ideas (transitions, rhetorical synthesis) and standard English conventions (grammar, punctuation).

Math

Algebra (linear equations and systems), advanced math (quadratics, exponentials, functions), problem-solving and data analysis (ratios, percentages, statistics) and geometry/trigonometry. A Desmos graphing calculator is built into the app for the whole math section — learning to use it well is one of the biggest score multipliers.

What is a good SAT score?

The average total score is roughly in the low 1000s. As a rule of thumb: 1200+ is competitive at many public universities, 1350+ is strong for selective colleges, and 1500+ is in range for the most selective. The best target is the middle 50% range published by each college on your list. Use our SAT ↔ ACT converter to compare scores.

8-week study plan

  1. Week 1 — Baseline: take a full-length official practice test in Bluebook under real timing. Log every miss by skill.
  2. Weeks 2–3 — Biggest gaps: drill the 2–3 skills that cost the most points. For most students: linear equations, grammar boundaries (commas/semicolons), and inference questions.
  3. Week 4 — Practice test #2 and update your error log.
  4. Weeks 5–6 — Mixed practice + Desmos: timed mixed sets; learn to graph systems and find intersections in Desmos.
  5. Week 7 — Practice test #3, pacing review (aim for ~1 min 11 s per R&W question and ~1 min 35 s per math question).
  6. Week 8 — Taper: light review, sleep, logistics (admission ticket, charged device, ID).

Free official practice: College Board’s Bluebook app has full-length adaptive practice tests, and Khan Academy offers free official SAT practice. Start there before paying for anything.

Frequently asked questions

Is the SAT still required for college?

Policies vary. Many colleges are test-optional, but a growing number of selective colleges have reinstated testing requirements. Check each college’s current admissions page.

How many times can I take the SAT?

As many times as you like. Many students take it 2–3 times; many colleges “superscore,” combining your best section scores across dates.

Can I use a calculator on the whole math section?

Yes. On the digital SAT a calculator is allowed on all math questions, and a Desmos graphing calculator is built into the testing app.

When should I take the SAT?

Most students take it for the first time in spring of junior year, leaving time to retest in summer or early fall of senior year.
