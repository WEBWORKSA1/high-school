How it works

  1. Pre-register below — we’ll email you when a round opens with the prompt, dates and prize pool.
  2. Create your entry during the round (usually 3–4 weeks).
  3. Judging by a panel of educators using published criteria: originality, clarity, accuracy and impact.
  4. Winners are announced on this page and receive prizes funded by that round’s sponsors and supporters.

Each round’s prize pool is confirmed and published before the round opens — we never announce a prize that isn’t funded. Entry is always free; no purchase necessary. Read the official rules.
