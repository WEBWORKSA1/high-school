Contest Official Rules

The rules that apply to every High.School contest round.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED. These rules apply to every High.School contest round unless a round’s announcement states additional terms, which will be published before the round opens.

1. Sponsor

Each round is administered by High.School (“Administrator”). Funding sponsors for a round, if any, are named in that round’s announcement.

2. Eligibility

Open to students enrolled in grades 9–12 (or the international equivalent) or in a recognized homeschool or online high school program, who are at least 13 years old at the time of entry. Entrants under the age of majority in their jurisdiction must have permission from a parent or legal guardian, who may be required to sign prize documents. Employees of the Administrator and round sponsors and their immediate families are not eligible. Residents of jurisdictions where the contest is prohibited or restricted are not eligible.

3. Entry period

Each round’s start and end dates and time zone are published in the round announcement. Late entries are not accepted.

4. How to enter

Follow the round instructions, which may include submitting an original work through the entry form. Limit one entry per person per round unless stated otherwise. Entries must be the entrant’s own original work, must not infringe anyone’s rights, and must follow any word or time limits. Use of AI tools is governed by the round announcement; undisclosed AI-generated work may be disqualified.

5. Judging

Entries are judged by a panel selected by the Administrator using the criteria published for the round (for example originality, clarity, accuracy and impact). Judges’ decisions are final. If no entry meets minimum standards, the Administrator may decline to award a prize.

6. Prizes

The prize(s), approximate retail value and number of winners for each round are published before the round opens, and only funded prizes are announced. Prizes are non-transferable; no substitution except by the Administrator, who may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes. Prize delivery may require completing an affidavit of eligibility and a publicity release (signed by a parent/guardian for minors).

7. Winner notification

Potential winners are notified by email (and, for minors, through the parent/guardian email provided) within 30 days of the round’s close. If a potential winner cannot be reached within 14 days or does not return required documents, an alternate may be selected.

8. License to entries

Entrants keep ownership of their work. By entering, entrants grant the Administrator a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display the entry and the entrant’s first name, grade and region on High.School and its channels in connection with the contest, with parent/guardian permission for minors.

9. Privacy

Personal information is used only to administer the contest and as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not sell entrant information.

10. General conditions

The Administrator may cancel, modify or suspend a round if fraud, technical failure or any other factor impairs its integrity, and may disqualify anyone who tampers with entry or violates these rules. The Administrator is not responsible for lost, late, incomplete or misdirected entries.

11. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, entrants release the Administrator and sponsors from liability arising from participation or acceptance and use of a prize.

12. Governing law & disputes

These rules are governed by the laws of the jurisdiction stated in the round announcement. Questions? Contact us.

13. Winners list

Winners are posted on the contests page after each round.
