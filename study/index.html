How to use the Study Hub

Each subject guide follows the same structure so you always know where to look: the typical course sequence (so you can plan ahead), the key topics teachers test most, a study method tailored to that subject, the mistakes that cost the most points, curated videos, and a 5-question quiz with explanations.

For the science of studying itself — retrieval practice, spacing and interleaving — read How to Study Effectively. To turn a study plan into a schedule, use the study timer and flashcards.
