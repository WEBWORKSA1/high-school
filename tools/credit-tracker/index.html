How many credits do you need to graduate?

Requirements are set by each state and district, and typically range from about 20 to 28 credits. A common pattern is 4 English, 3–4 math, 3 science, 3 social studies, 1–2 world language, 1 fine arts, 1–2 PE/health, plus electives. Some states add requirements such as personal finance, civics tests or community service. Behind on credits?

You have options: summer school, credit recovery courses, online courses, or dual enrollment. Our Online High School & Diploma guide explains each path and how to check accreditation.

Frequently asked questions

What is a credit?

Usually one credit equals a full-year course that meets daily; a semester course is often 0.5 credit. Some schools use different systems, so check your catalog.

Can I graduate early?

Many districts allow early graduation if all credits and tests are complete. Talk to your counselor by 10th grade to plan.
