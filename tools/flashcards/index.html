Why flashcards work (when used right)

Flashcards force retrieval practice — pulling information out of memory — one of the most effective study strategies known. Combine it with spacing (reviewing over several days) for long-term retention.

  • Say the answer out loud before flipping.
  • Keep cards small: one idea per card.
  • Shuffle so you can’t rely on order.
  • Delete cards you know cold; spend time on the hard ones.

Keyboard: ← → to move, Space to flip.
