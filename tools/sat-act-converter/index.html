About the concordance

College Board and ACT publish an official concordance that links scores on the two tests — it’s what colleges use to compare applicants. A 1250 SAT, for example, corresponds to a 26 ACT. Use it to decide which test you’re stronger on after taking a practice test of each.

Percentile bands are approximate and for orientation only; your official score report shows your exact percentiles. The ACT updated its format in 2025; colleges continue to use the concordance to compare scores. See SAT vs ACT.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 1200 SAT good?

A 1200 is above the national average and corresponds to about a 25 ACT. It’s competitive at many public universities.

Which is easier to improve, SAT or ACT?

It depends on your strengths. Many students see fast gains on ACT English or SAT Reading & Writing grammar questions because they rely on learnable rules.
