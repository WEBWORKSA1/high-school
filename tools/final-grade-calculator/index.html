The formula

If your final is worth w (as a decimal) of your grade, then: Needed = (Target − Current × (1 − w)) ÷ w. Example: current 86%, final worth 20%, target 90% → (90 − 86 × 0.8) ÷ 0.2 = 106%. That’s out of reach without extra credit — so the plan would be to aim for an 87–88% instead, or raise the current grade first.

Making the most of your final

  1. Start reviewing 10–14 days out with short daily sessions.
  2. Rank units by points at stake and by how weak you are; study the overlap first.
  3. Do practice tests under timed conditions — then review every miss.
  4. Sleep the night before; memory consolidates during sleep.

Frequently asked questions

What if I need more than 100%?

The target isn’t reachable with the final alone. Ask your teacher about extra credit, test corrections or replacement policies, or adjust your target.

What if my class uses points instead of percentages?

Convert to a percentage: points earned ÷ points possible × 100, and the final’s weight = final points ÷ total course points.
