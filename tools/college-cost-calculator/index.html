How this estimate works

We grow today’s net yearly cost (cost minus expected grants) by your inflation rate for each year until and during college, then add the years together. Savings grow monthly at your expected return. The gap is what loans, work-study, family income or additional savings would need to cover.

Ways to shrink the gap

  • Apply for aid every year — file the FAFSA and any required CSS Profile.
  • Stack scholarships — track them with our scholarship tracker.
  • Use each college’s Net Price Calculator — required on US college websites and often far lower than the sticker price.
  • Earn credit early through AP, IB or dual enrollment.
  • Consider community college transfer pathways for the first two years.

Educational estimate only — not financial advice. Investment returns are not guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good college cost inflation rate to assume?

Many planners use 3–5% per year. Use a higher rate to be conservative.

What’s a 529 plan?

A US tax-advantaged education savings account. Earnings grow tax-free when used for qualified education expenses. Plans vary by state.
