How weighted grades work

Teachers often group work into categories with different weights — for example Tests 40%, Quizzes 20%, Homework 20%, Labs 20%. Your grade is the weighted average: multiply each category score by its weight, add them, and divide by the total weight. Find your weights in the course syllabus or grade portal.

Because tests usually carry the most weight, a 10-point improvement on tests raises the class grade far more than perfect homework does. Use this calculator to test “what if” scenarios before deciding where to spend study time.

Frequently asked questions

What letter-grade scale does this use?

A common US scale: 93+ A, 90–92 A−, 87–89 B+, 83–86 B, 80–82 B−, and so on down to below 60 = F. Your school’s scale may differ.

What if weights don’t add to 100%?

The calculator normalizes them automatically, which matches how most grade portals handle partially graded terms.
