How the Pomodoro Technique works

Developed by Francesco Cirillo, the method breaks work into focused intervals — traditionally 25 minutes — separated by short breaks. After four sessions, take a longer break. The timer creates urgency, makes big assignments feel manageable, and builds a measurable habit.

Tips for better focus sessions

  • Write the single task for the session before you press Start.
  • Phone in another room — notifications cost minutes of refocus time each.
  • During breaks, move: stretch, refill water, look away from screens.
  • Pair sessions with flashcards or practice problems — active work, not re-reading.

Frequently asked questions

Is 25 minutes the right length?

It’s a good start. Many high schoolers move to 40–50 minute sessions for deep work like essays once focus improves. Use the custom settings.

Does the timer work in the background?

Yes, while this tab stays open. A short tone plays when a session ends.
