Editorial Policy

Effective October 2026.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

Our standards

  • Useful first: every page should answer a real question a student, parent or educator has.
  • Accurate: facts about tests, aid and requirements are checked against official sources (College Board, ACT, IBO, NMSC, StudentAid.gov, NCES, BLS, state education departments).
  • Plain English: we explain jargon and avoid hype.
  • Original: our guides and practice questions are written by our team, not copied.

How content is made

  1. Research from official and reputable sources.
  2. Drafting by the High.School Editorial Team.
  3. Review by an editor and, for subject content, a subject-matter reviewer.
  4. Publication with an “Updated” date.
  5. Scheduled reviews (at least yearly, and whenever a test or policy changes).

Use of AI

We may use AI-assisted tools for research support, drafting and editing. Every page is reviewed and edited by people, and facts are verified against sources before publishing.

Advertising & partnerships

Ads are served automatically by Google AdSense; we don’t write content to please advertisers. Partners and sponsors never pay for editorial coverage or rankings. Sponsored content, if any, is clearly labeled.

Corrections

Spotted an error? Use our contact form and choose “Feedback or correction.” We review every report and update pages promptly.
