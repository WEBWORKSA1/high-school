About High.School

Free, independent, built for every student.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

High.School is a free, independent command center for high school. We bring together what students, parents, adult learners and educators need most — study guides, test prep, practical tools, college and career planning, and honest guidance on diploma pathways — in one fast, ad-supported, no-sign-up website.

Our mission

Every student deserves the kind of clear, organized guidance that usually comes from an expensive counselor or a well-connected family. We want a 9th grader in any town, a parent navigating the FAFSA for the first time, or a 35-year-old finishing a diploma to find the answer in minutes.

What we do

  • Explain — plain-English guides to every core subject and test.
  • Equip — free calculators and study tools that save data only on your device.
  • Connect — optional, no-obligation matches with vetted tutoring, test-prep, counseling and online-school options.
  • Celebrate — student contests with sponsor-funded prizes.

How we make money (transparently)

High.School is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense, referral fees from some partner programs when families ask to be matched, sponsorships, and reader donations. Advertisers and partners never influence our editorial content. See our editorial policy.

Who we are

High.School is written and maintained by the High.School Editorial Team — writers and reviewers with backgrounds in teaching, tutoring, counseling and educational publishing. We welcome corrections and new contributors: join the team.

Independence

High.School is not a school and is not affiliated with any school, district, testing organization or government agency. See our trademark & copyright disclosure.

Contact

Questions, corrections or ideas? Contact us. For domain, sponsorship, advertising or partnership inquiries, visit web.works/contact.
