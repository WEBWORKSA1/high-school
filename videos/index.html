How to learn from videos (not just watch them)

  1. Watch once without notes; then rewatch and pause to write the key idea of each segment.
  2. After the video, close it and explain the topic out loud in 60 seconds.
  3. Do 3–5 practice problems or the quiz on the matching subject page.
