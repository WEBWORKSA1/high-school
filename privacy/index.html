Privacy Policy

Effective October 2026.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

This Privacy Policy explains how High.School (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use high.school and related pages (the “Site”). We keep collection minimal: most tools run entirely in your browser and store data only on your device.

1. Who the Site is for

The Site is intended for users aged 13 and older, parents and guardians, adult learners and educators. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our forms block submissions from users who identify as under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, please use our contact form and we will delete it.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you submit through forms (for example name, email, optional phone, role, grade, goals, location, messages, applications and pledges).
  • Tool data (GPA courses, flashcards, checklists, trackers) is stored in your browser’s local storage on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Automatically collected information by our advertising and hosting providers, such as IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and cookie identifiers.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your requests and provide matched resources you ask for.
  • With your consent, to introduce you to a relevant partner (for example a tutoring, test-prep, counseling or online-school provider) for the needs you selected.
  • To send newsletters or alerts you sign up for (unsubscribe any time).
  • To operate, secure and improve the Site, and to show advertising.

4. Minors (13–17)

For students under 18, our lead forms require confirmation that a parent or guardian approved the request, and we include the parent/guardian in follow-up where possible. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share a minor’s information with partners without parent/guardian approval.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this Site or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this Site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Choosing “Non-personalized only” in our cookie notice requests non-personalized ads. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may be shown a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform.

6. Service providers

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) serves the Site and may log technical request data.
  • Form delivery: FormSubmit relays form submissions to our inbox.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts.
  • Videos: YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode); YouTube receives data only when you choose to play a video.
  • Payments: if you donate, the payment provider you choose (e.g., PayPal, Stripe, Buy Me a Coffee) processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We never receive full card details.

7. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers above, with partners you ask to be matched with, when required by law, or to protect rights and safety. We do not sell personal information.

8. Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping, then delete them. Data in your browser stays until you clear it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal information, and to opt out of “sale” or “sharing” for targeted advertising. To make a request, use our contact form and choose “Privacy request.” We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is 100% secure, so please don’t submit sensitive information (such as ID or financial numbers) through our forms.

11. International users

Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

12. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above.

13. Contact

Questions? Use our contact form.
