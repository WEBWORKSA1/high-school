☀️ Summer Programs, Jobs & Projects for High Schoolers

How to spend high school summers well: pre-college programs, free and paid options, internships, jobs, volunteering, independent projects, and application timelines.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

Summer is your chance to explore an interest deeply, earn money, help your community or build something of your own. Colleges don’t expect an expensive program — they care what you did and learned.

Options at a glance

OptionCostBest for

Timeline

Many selective programs have deadlines from January to April. Start researching in the fall. Ask teachers for recommendations at least three weeks early.

Spotting value (and avoiding expensive fluff)

Be cautious with programs that charge high fees but admit almost everyone and promise admissions advantages. A summer job or a self-driven project is often just as valuable — and sometimes more.

Frequently asked questions

Do summer programs help college admissions?

Selective, free programs can be impressive, but what matters most is what you learned and did. A job or meaningful project can be equally strong.

I can’t afford a program. What should I do?

Work, volunteer, take free online courses, or start a project. These are fully respected by colleges.
