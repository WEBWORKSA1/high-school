🍎 Free Resources for High School Teachers & Counselors

Free, classroom-ready resources for high school teachers and counselors: how to use High.School tools in class, study-skills mini-lessons, college-planning materials and partnership options.

📅 Updated October 2026✍️ High.School Editorial Team✅ Editorial policy

High.School is free for classrooms. Here’s how teachers and counselors use it, plus ways to collaborate with us.

Use our tools in class

  • GPA and grade calculators: a quick lesson on weighted averages that students immediately care about.
  • Practice quizzes on every subject page as bell-ringers or exit tickets.
  • Study timer projected for focused work blocks.
  • Flashcards for vocabulary review stations.

Mini-lessons (10 minutes each)

  1. Retrieval practice vs re-reading — demo with a 2-minute quiz.
  2. Cornell notes — model with today’s content.
  3. Planning a big assignment backward from the due date.
  4. Reading a college’s middle-50% score range.

For counselors

Share the 4-year roadmap at registration time, the FAFSA guide at fall family nights, and the scholarship tracker with seniors.

Work with us

Teachers can write or review guides, suggest tools, or sponsor a class entry in our contests. Schools and organizations can explore partnerships.

Frequently asked questions

Is High.School free for schools?

Yes. All guides and tools are free to use in classrooms.

Do students need accounts?

No. Tools save data only in the student’s own browser.
