Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

High.School is not currently a registered charity, so donations are not tax-deductible. They directly fund the site’s operations, content and student prizes.

Can my business sponsor instead?

Yes — sponsorships come with recognition on contests, tools or newsletters. See Advertise & Partner.

Can I give in another way?

Absolutely: share High.School with a student or teacher, report errors, contribute a guide, or volunteer as a student ambassador.

💛 Why give?

Every guide and tool on High.School is free, with no paywalls and no sign-ups. Ads cover part of the cost; supporters cover the rest and fund student prizes.
